08 · Brand Voice

Clear, technical, accountable

Sazvar should sound like an experienced engineering partner who understands specifications, timelines, and commercial realities.

Write like this

“We evaluate suppliers against your technical and commercial requirements.”

“One accountable team coordinates the tender from scope review to submission.”

“Planned maintenance helps protect availability and reduce avoidable disruption.”

Avoid this

“We find the cheapest supplier for everything.”

“We provide end-to-end world-class solutions.”

“Our AMC guarantees zero downtime.”

Lead with outcomesThen explain scope and process.
Use evidenceStandards, timing, records, and verification.
Stay measuredNo hype or unsupported guarantees.
Sound humanDirect, respectful, and never casual.

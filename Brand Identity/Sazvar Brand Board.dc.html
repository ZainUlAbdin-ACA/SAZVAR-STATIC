USAGE NOTES

Documents stay on canvas white with a single teal rule under the letterhead. Deep teal carries hover, active and emphasis states only — roughly 2% of any layout.

Reference numbers, dates and quantities are always IBM Plex Mono, so scanned tender packs stay legible at fax-grade quality.

For Arabic and Urdu material, set the mark unchanged and mirror the layout; the gear is symmetrical and needs no RTL variant.

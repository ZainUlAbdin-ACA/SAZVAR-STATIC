What types of procurement can Sazvar manage?

We support general, technical, engineering, operational, and institutional sourcing requirements. The scope is reviewed against your specifications, quantities, timing, and delivery location.

Does Sazvar work with universities?

Yes. Procurement, civil, and solar support is offered to universities and educational institutions as well as industrial clients and other suitable facilities.

What is included in a boiler or turbine AMC?

The scope is agreed around the specific equipment and operating needs — planned inspection and service coordination, scheduling, and maintenance records. Parts, specialist tasks, and response arrangements must be agreed separately.

Can Sazvar support projects outside Pakistan?

Sazvar is based in Pakistan and intends to grow into GCC markets, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Qatar. Share the location and scope so feasibility can be assessed; we do not imply an established office or delivery presence in those markets.
